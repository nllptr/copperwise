# 04: Validate price spread and evidence rules

**What to build:** An inspectable prototype compares real and controlled listing distributions to establish honest price-spread calculations and reference-summary rules.

**Blocked by:** 02: Verify market identity and upstream observations.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Demonstrate quantity-weighted median and middle-50% band using differently sized listings, unit-price normalization, sparse stock, and extreme prices.
- [ ] Exclude bid-only listings from price statistics while including their units and extreme-price units in total stock.
- [ ] Record chosen quantile conventions and evidence thresholds, including when estimates or summaries are withheld.
- [ ] Explain the band as price spread rather than confidence, sales evidence, or guaranteed manipulation resistance.
