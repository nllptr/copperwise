# Copperwise

Copperwise helps casual sellers understand auction-house asking prices and available stock.

## Language

**Casual seller**:
A player selling items they already own who wants easy visibility into asking-price trends and available stock with minimal setup.

**Listing**:
An offer to sell an item at an advertised price. A listing is not evidence of a completed sale.

**Price trend**:
The direction and context of observed asking prices over time. It describes listings rather than predicting future sale prices.

**Supply**:
The quantity of an item offered in observed auction listings at a given time. It does not measure how much of that item players own or will list later.

**Price spread**:
The distribution of observed asking prices, weighted by listed item units. It does not express confidence in an estimate.
